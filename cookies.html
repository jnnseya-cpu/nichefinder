Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026. How we use cookies and similar browser-storage technologies.

01What we use today

The current prototype sets no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage — a similar technology — to run the product itself:

KeyPurposeClass
nf_wallet / nf_acu_balanceYour ACU balances (paid + free welcome)Strictly necessary
nf_ledgerACU transaction historyStrictly necessary
nf_saved · nf_history · nf_reportsSaved niches, search runs, generated assetsStrictly necessary
nf_active_project · nf_state_* · nf_memory_*Your open project and its build progressStrictly necessary
nf_deck_theme · nf_invitePresentation theme and referral codeFunctional

02At production launch

The production release will add: session cookies for sign-in (strictly necessary), and — only with your consent — analytics cookies to understand aggregate product usage. A consent banner will let you accept or decline non-essential cookies before any are set, and change your choice at any time.

03Managing storage

You can clear localStorage and cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that in the prototype this erases your wallet, saved niches, and generated documents, since nothing is stored server-side yet.
