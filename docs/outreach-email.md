# Outreach email — earning the first backlinks for the AI-agents guide

Goal: a few quality inbound links to
**https://nichefinderhq.com/what-are-ai-agents.html** — the single biggest lever
for a new page on a young domain.

**Rules that keep this working (and out of spam folders):**
- **Personalise the first line every time.** Reference a specific thing they
  published. A generic blast gets deleted and can hurt your domain's reputation.
- **Lead with value, not the ask.** You're offering a resource their audience
  would use; the link is their choice, not your demand.
- **Send from a real person's address** (e.g. your name @nichefinderhq.com), not
  a no-reply. Keep it plain text.
- **One follow-up max**, ~5–7 days later, then stop.
- **Target relevance, not size.** A 2,000-subscriber founder newsletter that
  covers AI tooling beats a 200,000 general list.
- **Never buy links or use link farms** — it's the fastest way to a penalty.

Where to send: founder/indie-hacker newsletters, startup-community curators
(Slack/Discord/Circle), university entrepreneurship centres, small-business blogs,
and "AI tools for founders" roundup authors. Also answer relevant questions on
Reddit/Indie Hackers/Quora where the guide genuinely helps (link only when it
adds value — not drive-by).
