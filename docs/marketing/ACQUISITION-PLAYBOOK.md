# NICHE FINDER — Organic Customer Acquisition Playbook

## The blunt diagnosis

Zero customers is **not a product problem — it is a distribution problem**.
The product is built and tested; it has never been deployed to a public URL and
payments have never been switched on. You cannot acquire a customer for a site
that does not exist. Before any tactic below matters, the product must be **live
on a domain with payments on** — that is the founder actions in `LAUNCH.md`
(Stripe account + AI key + one deploy). Everything here is what makes that live
site actually convert, done organically, with the AI agents already in the OS.

## The funnel we now have (built, in code)

1. **Top of funnel — Free Niche Score (new, on the homepage).** A first-time
   visitor types an idea + country and gets an instant scored preview with a
   verdict — **no signup, no card, no credits**. This is the single biggest
   organic lever: value before the ask. It captures email into the lead store
   and produces a **shareable result**, so every visitor can become a
   distribution node.
2. **Capture — lead store.** Emails from the free score and contact form land
   in `/v1/leads`. That is your launch list from day one.
3. **Activation — 100 free credits.** The free score routes into the real
   Search Canvas where the welcome credits fund a genuine country-grounded run.
4. **Revenue — £5 top-up.** Metered ACUs, real Stripe payment.
5. **Loop — SEO Autopilot + Growth Engine + referral code.** Content and
   share assets pull new visitors back to step 1.

## The AI agents already applied to acquisition

- **Free Niche Score agent** — instant homepage value, deterministic, offline-capable.
- **SEO Autopilot (Content Agent)** — self-publishes interlinked, cited, schema-tagged articles that rank and funnel to the free score.
- **Growth Engine (6 generators + 4 intelligence tools)** — posts, adverts, email sequences, landing pages, hashtags, video scripts + best-time/audience/performance intelligence, so you (and partners) produce a week of organic content in minutes.
- **Lead capture agent** — every free score and contact submission becomes a list entry.
- **Sentinel** — keeps the funnel clean (bots can't farm free scores or pollute the list).

## The 30-day organic plan (founder-executable, near-zero spend)

**Week 0 (launch):** deploy, payments on, buy the £5 Starter yourself, run 3 real
searches to confirm output quality. Post the Free Niche Score link once on your
own LinkedIn/X with your own scored idea as proof.

**Week 1 — seed the loop:** turn on SEO Autopilot (publishes daily). Use the
Growth Engine to generate 5 posts + 1 video script; post one per weekday at the
best-time the intelligence tool recommends. Every post ends with the free-score
link. Personally answer every reply.

**Week 2 — go where founders already are:** post genuinely useful answers (not
spam) in 3–5 communities where your users live — r/Entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness,
Indie Hackers, relevant country/diaspora founder groups, X build-in-public. Lead
with a free score of *their* idea, not a pitch.

**Week 3 — partners:** give 5 incubators/accelerators/creators a partner link.
The Growth Engine lets each produce co-branded promotion from inside the OS.
Their audience is your audience.

**Week 4 — measure and double down:** read the lead store and campaign analytics;
whichever channel produced signups, do 3× more of it. Email the list with the
"full breakdown" the free score promised.

## The one number to watch

**Free scores run → emails captured → free searches → first £5.** If the top of
that funnel (free scores) is moving and the bottom (£5) isn't, the product needs
tuning. If the top isn't moving, it's pure distribution — post more, in more
places, with the free score as the hook. The instrumentation is in the lead
store and campaign analytics.

## The honest bottom line

The product can sell. It has never had the chance because it was never in front
of a human. Ship it, put the free score in front of people where they already
gather, and let the AI content agents keep the funnel fed. Acquisition is a
distribution problem now — and distribution starts the hour the site is live.
