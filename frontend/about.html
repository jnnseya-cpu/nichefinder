Venture creation shouldn't require venture capital.

Niche Finder exists for the operator with £10,000 or less and the ambition to build something real — in Kinshasa, London, Lagos, or anywhere the data reaches.

THE PROBLEMGood ideas die in the fog

Most first-time founders don't fail at execution — they fail at selection. They pick opportunities with no demand evidence, misjudge local competition, or commit capital to ideas that were never buildable at their budget. The research that would have told them costs more than the venture itself.

THE ANSWERAn operating system, not a search engine

Niche Finder compresses the venture-selection process into a disciplined loop: discover country-grounded opportunities matched to your available capital, score them deterministically (Market Readiness, Competitive Gap, Prospect of Success), and build the investor pack — market validation report, 3-year forecast and P&L, risk heatmap, business plan, and a 12-slide investor pitch deck — in one workspace.

Every number the system produces is internally consistent: the forecast in your pitch deck is the same model as your Excel export, anchored to the same assumptions.

PRINCIPLESWhat we hold ourselves to

  • Country first. An opportunity that ignores your market is a guess, not a plan.
  • Capital honesty. Every opportunity is matched and priced to your capital bracket — we show what you can realistically start and fund, whether that's under £10k or well above.
  • Deterministic scoring. The same inputs always produce the same verdict — no vibes.
  • You decide. The OS narrows the field and stress-tests the plan; the final call is always the operator's.
Niche Finder's scores, projections, and generated documents are decision-support tools — not financial advice, and not a guarantee of outcomes.
