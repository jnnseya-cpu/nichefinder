Venture Demand Observatory

See where entrepreneurial demand is forming — by country and sector — for SME programmes, development finance, and evidence-based policy. Delivered as a secured, privacy-by-design engagement.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN. The Observatory reports aggregated, anonymised metrics only — no personal data, no individual searches, no user identities. Any cell covering fewer than 20 operators is suppressed (k-anonymity, k = 20).

What the Observatory reports

Derived from the platform's own anonymised venture-discovery activity, scoped to your country or region, and delivered as a secured dashboard or data feed under an enterprise engagement.

Sector demand

Share of venture-discovery activity across the five-sector model — where founders are actually looking.

Adoption velocity

How discovery converts into deep validation over time — a leading indicator of formation intent.

Geographic intensity

Regional demand bands, so programmes target where near-ready operators actually are.

Capital & viability

Median capital sought and the distribution of viability scores — the readiness curve of the pipeline.

Policy brief

A plain-language read of the binding constraints (e.g. first-cheque finance gaps), generated from the aggregates.
