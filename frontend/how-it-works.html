Platform · How It Works

From one search to an investor-ready venture.

Niche Finder is the AI Operating System for Venture Creation. Every opportunity it surfaces is buildable under £10,000 / $10,000 / €10,000, scored deterministically, and backed by generated financials, plans, and pitch materials.

STEP 01Discover

Enter your country (mandatory — every result is grounded in your market) plus optional skills, interests, and capital. The AI Search Canvas returns ranked niche opportunities, each capped at the £10k class so it stays realistically fundable. Each search consumes 125 ACU.

STEP 02Score

Every opportunity carries a deterministic score out of 10 — green (8–10), amber (5–7), red (0–4) — built from three weighted pillars:

  • Market Readiness (PRS) — 35%: is demand real and current?
  • Competitive Gap (CS) — 30%: is there room to win?
  • Prospect of Success (PSS) — 35%: can you execute it here, now, under £10k?

The overall score maps to a decision: STRONG GO ≥ 7.5, CONDITIONAL GO 6.0–7.4, NO GO below 6.0. Breakthrough Discovery mode adds a fourth pillar — Breakthrough Potential (BPS) — and only labels an opportunity a breakthrough when both BPS and PSS clear 7.5.

STEP 03Build

Unlock a full opportunity (150 ACU) to open its Deep-Dive workspace, then execute engines in the Build Hub: market validation, 3-year cashflow forecast, risk heatmap, business plan, investor pitch deck, and the Excel + PDF export pack. Each output is investor-grade, branded, and versioned in your repository. All prices shown are bracket-1 (≤ £10k) figures — every action on the platform, from unlock and validation to documents and exports, scales with your capital bracket, not just search.

The Search Canvas shows your bracket live as you move the capital slider, and every price on screen updates before you commit. Unlocked projects carry their bracket into the Build Hub.

Every price is shown up front, before you commit — no hidden fees, no surprises. You choose your package; unused paid ACUs stay in your wallet.

Pay your way. Top up by card via Stripe, or by mobile money via KODA (Orange Money, M-Pesa) — charged in your local currency at the live exchange rate, shown before you confirm.

Grow together. The Growth Partner programme rewards real referrals: when someone you invite makes a paid purchase, you earn a commission in ACU. No reward for sign-ups alone — only genuine paid usage.

Capital parity: GBP, EUR, and USD zones use a flat £10k / €10k / $10k ceiling. Dollarized economies use $10k; all other regions use the local-currency equivalent of $10,000.
