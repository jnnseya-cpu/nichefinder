Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026. How we use cookies and similar browser-storage technologies.

01What we use today

Niche Finder sets no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage — a similar technology — and a sign-in token to run the product itself:

KeyPurposeClass
nf_session · nf_accountYour signed-in session token and account profileStrictly necessary
nf_wallet / nf_acu_balanceYour ACU balances (paid + free welcome)Strictly necessary
nf_ledgerACU transaction historyStrictly necessary
nf_saved · nf_history · nf_reportsSaved niches, search runs, generated assetsStrictly necessary
nf_active_project · nf_state_* · nf_memory_*Your open project and its build progressStrictly necessary
nf_deck_theme · nf_invitePresentation theme and referral codeFunctional

02Sign-in and analytics

Signed-in sessions are kept using a strictly-necessary token stored in your browser. We may add — only with your consent — analytics cookies to understand aggregate product usage; a consent banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies before any are set, and change your choice at any time.

03Managing storage

You can clear localStorage and cookies through your browser settings at any time. Clearing them removes locally-cached copies and preferences and signs you out. Your account, ACU wallet, and generated documents are stored on our servers and are restored when you sign in again.

Questions: privacy@nichefinderhq.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
